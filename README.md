# Tunes

A Spotify-style music player for Windows for the music on your PC.

<img src="icon.png" width="120">

## 📱 Android: [Download Tunes for Android (Tunes.apk)](https://github.com/tinkerkid05-wq/Tunes/releases/latest/download/Tunes.apk)

Tap the link above in **Chrome**, then open the download and tap **Install**. If Android asks, allow installs from Chrome.
It plays the music saved on your phone.

## 💻 Windows

1. Download **[Tunes.exe](../../releases/latest/download/Tunes.exe)** from the latest release.
2. Run it. It's one file, with nothing to install, on Windows 10 or 11.
   Windows may say it "protected your PC" because the app isn't signed: click **More info → Run anyway**.
3. Click the folder icon (top right) and pick the folder with your music.

Tunes checks for new versions once a day and asks before updating itself.

## Features

- Library, playlists, Liked Songs, albums, search, queue, shuffle and repeat
- Synced lyrics, a fullscreen visualizer, crossfade, speed control, sleep timer and a mini player
- Download songs and playlists from YouTube, and import Spotify playlists
- Automatic cover art, Discord "Listening to" status, and taskbar controls

Your music, playlists and settings stay on your own PC.
